# CS2 skin trading bot — research

Research and backtesting only. No real-money trading.

## Goal

Collect the full history of CS2 skin prices and sales, replay it to test trading
strategies, and find out whether a systematic approach makes money after all real
costs and constraints (marketplace fees, trade locks, liquidity).

## Questions to answer

- Which strategies, if any, actually hold up?
- In which market conditions do they work? Strategies should cover both rising and
  falling markets. The market is falling now, so downturn behaviour matters most.
- Do they beat simply holding skins?
- If nothing beats the costs, say so honestly.

## Constraints

- Technical decisions are mine to make; propose an approach and recommend with reasons.
- Research and testing only.
