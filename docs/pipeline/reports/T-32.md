## Choices made (also `forward.CHOICES`: printed by `cs2 forward` and listed under the report's forward table)
- Bar stamp: bar h = each item's newest ask and bid with feed_time in (h − 1h, h]. The contract says "hour start"; this is what the DoD's "never sees feed_time > h" needs. Orders decided at h fill on the next hour with a snapshot
- Missing snapshot hour: no bar, no step; stale quotes are not carried, pending orders wait
- Price = ask (bid when no ask); sales give only `volume` = sales in the hour already fetched at h, else 0. Sale prices (RUB) are not used
- Fills: buy at the ask, sell at the bid, no ratio, no spread penalty, 1 unit per item and side per hour (only the best quote is known, as in T-47); a missing quote → `no_data`. Observed sales do not cap fills
- Holdings are valued at the newest bar's bid (ask when nobody bids) less the seller fee
- Lock = 168 hours from the fill hour. Day parameters stay calendar days; parameters that count bars count hours (mean_reversion's minimum sample, buy-and-hold's 10-decision retry window)
- Universe, category index and regime come from Steam daily bars of UTC days that ended before the decision hour (28 days of snapshots cannot give 30-day history or liquidity)
- Report: a separate "Forward paper test" section with adjacent `backtest` and `forward` columns (same strategy, params, dates), read from `data/forward/results.parquet`; it is not in the results table, so not in the hash
## Findings
- `cs2 forward --strategy S [--from DT] [--capital 100,1000] [--params JSON] [--min-days 28]`; saves each run under `data/runs/<run_id>` (`freq: "1h"`) and runs the same-dates daily backtest for the report column (skipped with a message when Steam bars do not cover the dates)
- Also changed, outside the task's file list: `backtest/engine.py` (`quotes` flag, hourly steps) and `backtest/view.py` (`MarketData.view`, `version`); the engine at `freq="1h"` cannot be done elsewhere. Hourly run_id = spec + data_version + exact hours + `snapshot_version()`
- Real data: sales exist for one day only (2026-11-09, 120 items), so hourly `volume` is ~0 everywhere; that is why fills are not capped by it. Asks and bids have different feed_times; 1–2 snapshots per hour
- Not point-in-time by `fetched_at` for asks/bids: a snapshot is fetched ~1–2 min after its feed_time, so a feed at h − 1 min counts as known at h
- Performance risk for the real run: every decision concatenates up to 45 days of hourly frames for all ~27,000 items (≈ 29 M rows). Restrict bars to the Steam universe plus held items before running on 28 days, or the Mac will lag
- 1 unit per item per hour makes a $1,000 run slow to deploy capital in cheap items; expect many `volume_cap` fills
- `Store.data_version()` / `snapshot_version()` now use `fetchall()`: an open `fetchone()` result made the next `db.sql()` fail in duckdb 2.0
- Sharpe is not shown for forward runs: `metrics()` annualises by √365 and hourly returns would overstate it
