## Assumptions made (also in the `arbitrage.py` docstring and printed by the command)
- Decision price: the last Steam daily bar of a UTC day before the snapshot's day, ≤ 3 days old; the same day's bar is never used. "Known at that hour" is by bar date, not by when we fetched it
- Edge = (Steam / 1.15) / ask − 1; buy fee 0%; asks under $0.20 skipped (Steam's $0.01 fee floors); buy only at edge ≥ 5%
- Size: 1 unit per item per snapshot (only the lowest ask is known), per item per day ≤ 10% of the reference bar's Steam volume
- Lock: bought on day d → sold on d + 8 (first full day after the 7-day lock) at that day's Steam bar / 1.15; no bar → wait up to 3 days, then unsold = 0. Sale-day volume is not re-checked, units above the share are counted
- Capital: C revolves over 8 days → spend ≤ C / 8 per UTC day ($12.50 and $125), no carry-over. Proceeds are wallet money, so each cycle needs fresh cash; gains are not reinvested
- Daily profit = mean over resolved buy days (sale day inside the Steam data) of that day's proceeds − cost
